Hello World! I’m Jun Shen, though some people call me J.

This is the first post on my blog, and honestly I’m pretty excited about finally putting this out.

I’ve always had a lot of thoughts, ideas, and random things I wanted to share, but most of it just stayed in my head. So instead of letting everything pile up, I figured I’d put it all here. A place where I can share what I’m thinking, what I’m learning, and the things I’m building as I go.

This site will also have sections for Projects and Experience, which I’m really looking forward to building out.

Projects

The Projects section will basically be my little launchpad where I post updates on whatever I’m currently working on, experimenting with, or trying to figure out. I’ve got a few fun UI ideas I want to test there, so I’m excited to get that live soon.

Experience
